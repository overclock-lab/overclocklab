FAQ

Questions, answered.

A practical overview of websites, automation, enquiries, payment setup, content and project delivery.

Do you provide website hosting?

Yes. We can help with deployment and hosting setup. The exact hosting provider and recurring hosting cost depend on the project.

Can you connect n8n with my existing tools?

In many cases, yes. The available approach depends on whether the tool provides an API, webhook, email integration or another supported connection.

Can you automate enquiries from a website?

Yes. A typical flow can capture the enquiry, store it, notify the right person and create a follow-up task or message.

Do you build payment pages?

Yes. We can build the page and customer journey around your chosen payment provider. Provider fees and account requirements remain separate.

Do you edit short videos?

Yes. We can handle short-form edits, captions, text overlays, pacing, basic motion graphics and platform-ready exports.

How does pricing work?

The pricing page gives starting references. Final pricing depends on scope, number of pages, integrations, content volume, complexity and support.

How do I start?

Use the enquiry form or contact us on WhatsApp. A short description of your requirement is enough for the first discussion.

Do you offer post-launch support?

Yes. Support and maintenance can be included or arranged separately depending on the project.
