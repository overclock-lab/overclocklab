Privacy Policy

Your information should be handled responsibly.

This starter privacy policy is written for the current static website and should be reviewed before production use, especially if you connect analytics, CRM, email or third-party form services.

1. Information you provide

If you submit the enquiry form, you may provide your name, email, phone number, service requirement, budget and message.

2. Browser storage

The current frontend stores the latest submitted enquiry in your browser's localStorage so the site can retain the submission on that device. This does not itself send the information to a server.

3. WhatsApp and email

If you choose to contact Overcloud Labs through WhatsApp or email, your information is handled by those respective services under their own policies.

4. Third-party services

Future integrations such as analytics, payment providers, CRM systems or automation platforms may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact mail.getlive@gmail.com.
